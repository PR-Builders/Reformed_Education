Second Presbyterian Church (PCA), Greenville, SC105 River Street, Greenville, South Carolina · Presbyterian Church in America A founding congregation of the PCA (1973) that co-hosted the PCA’s 43rd General Assembly in 2013, has planted three daughter churches and supports more than 20 missionary families. Its sermons are on SermonAudio. Sunday worship is at 8:30 and 11:00 am with an evening service at 6:00 pm, and sermons are on SermonAudio and YouTube. First Presbyterian Church (ARP), Columbia, SC1324 Marion Street, Columbia, South Carolina · Associate Reformed Presbyterian Church Downtown Columbia’s oldest church, left the Presbyterian Church in the United States in 1983 to join the ARP. Its building and graveyard are on the National Register of Historic Places. Its sermon archive on SermonAudio is large (233 pages of sermons), offers audio, podcast and webcast, and shows current sermons from September 2026; series include ‘Ephesians: Life in Christ’ and ‘A Study in Zechariah’. Covenant Community Orthodox Presbyterian Church (OPC), Greenville area, SC776 S. Batesville Rd., Greer, South Carolina (Greenville area). Sunday worship is at 10:30 am and 5:00 pm with Sunday School at 9:15 am (times as listed from August 23, 2026), streamed on YouTube, and its SermonAudio page has 1,969 sermons. Christ Covenant Church (PCA), Matthews, NCMatthews, North Carolina (Charlotte area) · Presbyterian Church in America ‘A reformed congregation of the Presbyterian Church in America committed to making disciples who worship God, grow in community, love others, and serve the world.’ It has 822 sermons on SermonAudio and services on Sunday at 10:45 am and 6:00 pm. Tenth Presbyterian Church (PCA), Philadelphia, PA12th and Walnut Streets, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · Presbyterian Church in America Historic center-city church known for consistent expository preaching and the Bible Study Hour program of James Montgomery Boice. First Presbyterian Church (PCA), Jackson, MSJackson, Mississippi · Presbyterian Church in America One of the PCA’s founding congregations and, by reports, the largest Presbyterian congregation in Mississippi.
