Clinical and Professional Care

If you or someone you know is in danger or thinking about suicide, call your local emergency number now or, in the U.S., call or text 988. Biblical counseling is not a substitute for medical or clinical care.

Christian Association for Psychological Studies (CAPS)Organization · Founded 1956 Association of Christian psychologists, counselors, therapists, social workers, researchers and students (more than 1,200 members in the U.S., Canada and 25+ other countries), publishing the Journal of Psychology and Christianity and holding an annual conference. Mental HealthBiblical Counseling International OCD Foundation: What Is OCD and Scrupulosity?Article · International OCD Foundation Secular, evidence-based explanation of scrupulosity (religious or moral OCD), which the page says affects people of all faiths, and of its main treatment: cognitive behavior therapy with exposure and response prevention (ERP), with medication in some cases. Mental Health Scrupulosity and OCD Information Packet for Faith LeadersArticle · International OCD Foundation 20-page packet for clergy compiled from IOCDF, ADAA and AnxietyCanada material: what OCD and scrupulosity are, ERP, how accommodating and giving reassurance can feed OCD, and questions for clergy.
