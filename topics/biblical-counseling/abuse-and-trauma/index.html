Abuse and Trauma

If you or someone you know is in danger or thinking about suicide, call your local emergency number now or, in the U.S., call or text 988. Biblical counseling is not a substitute for medical or clinical care.
Safety. If you are being hurt, or are afraid of someone at home, call your local emergency number. In the U.S., the National Domestic Violence Hotline is free, confidential and open 24/7: 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788. A book is not a safety plan.
