Biblical Counseling and Mental Health

Helping people who struggle, and finding help yourself. Biblical counseling is not a substitute for medical or clinical care; in an emergency, call your local emergency number or, in the U.S., call or text 988.

If you or someone you know is in danger or thinking about suicide, call your local emergency number now or, in the U.S., call or text 988. Biblical counseling is not a substitute for medical or clinical care.
