Church

First Presbyterian Church (PCA), Jackson, MS

Theological posture

Confessional Reformed

Holds a Reformed confession (the Westminster Standards, the Three Forms of Unity or the 1689 Baptist Confession) as its standard.

Doctrinal basis. PCA congregation.

Points to weigh

  • The church has issued a public statement on its own history regarding race; see byFaith for details.
  • The claim that it is the state’s largest Presbyterian congregation is from a summary of public sources.

Classification and points to weigh are reformededucation.org's judgments from the sources listed below. They describe and do not endorse; confirm current details with the institution.

About

One of the PCA’s founding congregations and, by reports, the largest Presbyterian congregation in Mississippi. It played a significant role in establishing the PCA, Reformed Theological Seminary and Reformed University Ministries.
