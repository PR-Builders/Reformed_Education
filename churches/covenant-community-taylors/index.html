Church

Covenant Community Orthodox Presbyterian Church (OPC), Greenville area, SC

Theological posture

Confessional Reformed

Holds a Reformed confession (the Westminster Standards, the Three Forms of Unity or the 1689 Baptist Confession) as its standard.

Doctrinal basis. States that it grounds its doctrine in the Westminster Confession and Catechisms and is governed by elders, as a local congregation of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church.

Points to weigh

  • The church’s own site gives a Greer address, while its SermonAudio page still gives the Taylors address; confirm the location before visiting.
  • Its own pages did not say whether it sings only psalms in worship.

Classification and points to weigh are reformededucation.org's judgments from the sources listed below. They describe and do not endorse; confirm current details with the institution.

About

OPC congregation in the Greenville area that says ‘we preach Christ and Him crucified’ and emphasizes Reformation Christianity. Sunday worship is at 10:30 am and 5:00 pm with Sunday School at 9:15 am (times as listed from August 23, 2026), streamed on YouTube, and its SermonAudio page has 1,969 sermons.
