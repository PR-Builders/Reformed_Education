Life and Books and Everything

Theological posture

Reformed heritage

Reformed in heritage or emphasis, but its confessional commitment is broader, not stated or not checked; read the points to weigh.

Doctrinal basis. Published by Clearly Reformed. The page reviewed does not state a confession or denomination for the podcast.

Points to weigh

  • Covers current events and ‘hot topics’ as well as theology, so its political and cultural comment reflects the hosts’ views rather than a church’s position.

Classification and points to weigh are reformededucation.org's judgments from the sources listed below. They describe and do not endorse; confirm current details with the institution.

About

Kevin DeYoung’s conversational podcast with Justin Taylor and Collin Hansen on faith, theology, history, current events and books; long-form episodes, often with a guest.
