Seminary

Covenant Theological Seminary

Theological posture

Confessional Reformed

Trustees and faculty are documented as subscribing to a Reformed confession (the Westminster Standards, the Three Forms of Unity or similar).

Doctrinal basis. Faculty must subscribe to the Westminster Standards (Wikipedia).

Points to weigh

  • 2018: the Revoice conference met at Memorial Presbyterian Church in St. Louis. The seminary's president said Covenant was not an official sponsor, but a seminary vice president spoke there. In March 2019 the president announced that no faculty would take part in the 2019 conference (Christian Post; byFaith).

Classification and points to weigh are reformededucation.org's judgments from the sources listed below. They describe and do not endorse; confirm current details with the institution.

About

PCA seminary; its M.A. in Biblical and Theological Studies can be completed fully online.
