Reading guide notes
Rated Moderate by reformededucation.org. Reviewers describe the Systematic Theology (three volumes) as clearly written but a substantial academic work, and his Romans commentary as accessible to educated laypeople and a good way into his thinking. This is a judgment from those descriptions, not a measurement.
