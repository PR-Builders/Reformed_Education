About Reformed Education

Reformed education and resources, in one place.

Purpose

Reformed Education is intended to become a comprehensive directory and resource hub for Reformed Christian education: seminaries, colleges, Christian schools, online courses, catechisms, books, lectures, podcasts, study resources, games and quizzes.

Current status

This is the initial frontend foundation. The site's structure, search and study tools are in place, but directory entries, catechism text and quiz questions are placeholders. No institution, denomination, accreditation, statistic or theological text has been added yet; each will be added only from verified or approved sources.

Editorial principles

  • Never invent institutions, URLs, statistics, quotations or theological claims.
  • Where information is missing, say so plainly: [Placeholder — information to be added].
  • Listing an entry is not an endorsement.
  • Link to original sources and describe them rather than copying them. See Sources & Copyright.
