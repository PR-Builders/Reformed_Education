Sources & Copyright

Reformed Education is a catalog, not a copy. We point you to original sources, describe them, and credit them.

How this site treats content

  • We link, summarize and describe. Entries consist of metadata, short descriptions and links to the original organization or publisher.
  • We do not republish copyrighted works — books, courses, lectures, articles, study guides or catechism editions still under copyright — without permission.
  • Public-domain works may be presented in full, but their source, edition and translation are still identified.
  • Quotations are brief and always carry the author and source. The site caps displayed quotations at 300 characters.
  • Linking is not endorsement. Please visit the original source for the full work and for current, authoritative details.

Confessions and catechisms

Every catechism or confession records the specific edition and translation its text comes from (for example, a named publisher's or denomination's edition), along with that edition's copyright or public-domain status. Until an approved edition is chosen, the text on this site is placeholder content.
